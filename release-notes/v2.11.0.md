# DevTools v2.11.0

## Highlights

- New PDF Tools: merge PDFs, split or extract pages, reorder, rotate, and delete pages, turn images into a PDF, and save pages as images. Your files stay on this device.
- New Email Header Analyzer: paste the headers of a message to see each server it passed through, the delay at each hop, and the SPF, DKIM, and DMARC results.
- SVG to CSS now shrinks your SVG with SVGO before it makes the data URI.

## New Tools

- PDF Tools shows every page of the new document in order. Drag pages or use the arrow buttons to reorder them, and rotate or delete pages. Select pages by clicking or by typing ranges such as `1-3, 5, 8-`. Save one PDF, split the pages into separate PDFs in a .zip file, or save the pages as PNG or JPEG at 72, 150, or 300 DPI. Images can go on pages that fit the image, or on A4 or Letter pages. The new PDF keeps no title, author, or other metadata from the source files.
- Email Header Analyzer lists the hops in delivery order with the sending IP, the protocol, and the delay at each hop, and marks slow hops. It shows the SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and ARC results that your mail server recorded, and warns when replies go to another domain. Open a .eml or .msg file, or paste the headers on the home screen to open the tool.

## Tool Improvements

- SVG to CSS has an Optimize with SVGO switch, on by default. It shortens paths and numbers, merges shapes, and removes hidden elements, and it keeps the viewBox and title. The tool shows how much smaller the SVG became. Turn the switch off to keep the original structure.
- Large Mermaid flowcharts and DB diagrams can zoom out far enough to fit the canvas.
- HTML Preview fits very wide pages in a narrow pane.
