# DevTools v2.7.1

## Highlights

- A new Settings window keeps your preferences in one place. Open it from the gear in the sidebar, or press Cmd+, (Ctrl+, on Windows and Linux).
- The sidebar footer is now one compact row. A blue badge shows only when an update needs your attention.
- The theme can follow your computer again. Choose System, Light, or Dark in Settings.

## App Updates

- Settings has four sections: General, Updates, Shortcuts, and About.
- General lets you choose if the sidebar shows at launch and if DevTools suggests a tool when you paste. You can also clear your recent tools and favorites.
- Updates shows the update status, the update button, and the "Update automatically" switch.
- Shortcuts holds the keyboard shortcut settings that used to open from the sidebar.
- Click the version number in the sidebar to see What's New.
- The keyboard shortcut rows are aligned, and long shortcuts no longer get cut off.
