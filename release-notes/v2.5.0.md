# DevTools v2.5.0

## Highlights

- Video Metadata Cleaner removes AI labels (C2PA Content Credentials and XMP), GPS positions, camera details, recording dates, and app names from MP4, MOV, M4V, 3GP, MKV, and WebM videos. It does not re-encode, so the picture, the sound, and the file size stay the same.
- The tool also removes timed metadata tracks, such as GoPro telemetry and iPhone face detection data, and clears their data in the file.
- After cleaning, the tool reads the clean copy again and lists anything that is left. Videos stay on your device and are never uploaded.

## Tool Improvements

- Image Metadata Cleaner now names AI video tools, such as Sora, Veo, Runway, Kling, Pika, and Luma, when they appear in metadata.

## App Updates

- The AI Tools section now has 5 tools.
